# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for an unpatched vulnerability, and do not submit it as a case.

Report it privately to security@shawsilicon.ai. Include what you found, how to reproduce
it, and which vendor, project or specification it affects.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge that we received it.
2. We confirm whether it reproduces.
3. If a third party owns the affected design, tool or specification, the report goes to
   them and the disclosure timeline is theirs to set, not ours.
4. A case is published only after the owner has disclosed, or has declined to act and the
   agreed embargo has passed.

We will not publish a finding that puts working systems at risk in order to have a more
interesting case. A case that cannot be published safely stays unpublished.

## Scope

This policy covers findings that arrive through this repository. Vulnerabilities in
ShawSilicon's own products and services are also reportable to the same address.
